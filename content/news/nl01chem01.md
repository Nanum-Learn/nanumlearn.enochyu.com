Hi, we are very excited to share that we will be hosting our first
Nanum session starting November 3rd! NL01CHEM01 will be lead
by our Vice President William Kang, and we have very excited
activities prepared as well!

We hope to see 6th to 9th grade students interested in exploring
more into Cheimstry. Our sessions will be held **every Tuesday
10 PM - 11 PM (GMT+9) for four weeks**. Below are some of the
contents that we will discuss in our session.

* Week 1:
  * Classifying matter, physical and chemical changes, and density.
  * Atomic structure, isotopes, and ions.
* Week 2:
  * Periodic table patterns, valence electrons, and common ion charges.
  * Ionic and covalent bonding, chemical formulas, and simple Lewis structures.
* Week 3:
  * Chemical reactions, balancing equations, and conservation of mass.
  * The mole, molar mass, and grams-to-moles conversions.
* Week 4:
  * Solutions, concentration, and dilution.
  * Review and application through an integrated chemistry problem.

If any of these topics excites you, please
**[submit this form](https://forms.gle/wCS4RWX9rFAkTBMU7)** to
register for NL01CHEM01. We are very excited to see you all,
and see you next week!
